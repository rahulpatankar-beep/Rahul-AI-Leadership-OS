# AI Governance

Build an enterprise governance system spanning policy, inventory, risk, controls, assurance, auditability, accountability, third-party AI, lifecycle governance, and executive oversight.

## Anchor frameworks
- NIST AI RMF
- ISO/IEC 42001
- ISO/IEC 23894
- EU AI Act
- OECD AI principles
- OWASP GenAI guidance

NIST's AI Resource Center is designed to operationalize AI RMF through technical guidance, profiles, crosswalks, use cases, and TEVV resources. Keep this folder evidence-driven and version-aware.
