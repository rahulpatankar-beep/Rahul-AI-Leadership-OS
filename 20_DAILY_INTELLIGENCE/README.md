# Rahul AI Leadership Daily Intelligence System

## Daily brief structure

1. Top 10 AI developments
2. Five enterprise AI use cases
3. AI Operations developments
4. Agentic AI developments
5. Governance and Responsible AI
6. Regulation Watch
7. Three research papers worth knowing
8. Market Intelligence
9. Executive Insight
10. Rahul Action

## Rahul Action format

Every brief should end with one action in each category when justified:

- **LEARN** — capability to study
- **BUILD** — artifact or architecture to create
- **APPLY** — behavior/framework to use
- **PUBLISH** — thought-leadership idea

## Daily workflow

`Scan → Select → Understand → Translate → Capture → Apply → Publish`

## Human effort target

15–30 minutes per day, 45–60 minutes per week, 60–90 minutes per month.

Automation should remove collection, filtering, deduplication, summarization, and formatting. Human judgment remains responsible for strategic interpretation, prioritization, architecture decisions, and personal point of view.
