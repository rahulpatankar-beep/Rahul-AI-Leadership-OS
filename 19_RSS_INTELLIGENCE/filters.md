# RSS Filtering Rules

## High-priority keywords

### Executive
`AI strategy`, `AI transformation`, `CAIO`, `chief AI officer`, `AI operating model`, `enterprise AI`, `AI ROI`, `AI adoption`, `AI maturity`

### Operations
`LLMOps`, `MLOps`, `AgentOps`, `AIOps`, `AI observability`, `AI evaluation`, `AI monitoring`, `AI FinOps`, `AI SRE`, `model lifecycle`

### Agents
`agentic AI`, `AI agents`, `multi-agent`, `MCP`, `tool use`, `agent evaluation`, `agent memory`, `agent security`, `digital worker`

### Governance
`AI governance`, `AI RMF`, `ISO 42001`, `AI risk`, `AI assurance`, `AI audit`, `AI inventory`, `AI impact assessment`, `responsible AI`

### Security
`LLM security`, `prompt injection`, `agent security`, `AI red teaming`, `AI threat`, `adversarial AI`, `model security`, `AI supply chain`

### Regulation
`EU AI Act`, `GPAI`, `high-risk AI`, `AI regulation`, `AI compliance`, `India AI`, `MeitY`, `AI standards`

### Research
`reasoning`, `multimodal`, `agents`, `RAG`, `evaluation`, `inference`, `small language models`, `synthetic data`, `AI safety`, `world models`

## Down-rank / ignore

- Generic funding announcements with no strategic implication
- Pure product marketing with no architecture or implementation evidence
- Repeated model benchmark stories with no enterprise relevance
- Celebrity/viral AI stories
- Low-quality AI-generated news summaries
