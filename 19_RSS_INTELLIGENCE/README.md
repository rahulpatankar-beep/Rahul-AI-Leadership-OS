## Priority

- **P1:** Must-read / primary / standards / major enterprise research

## Source-selection rule

Prefer primary sources, standards bodies, official regulators, research institutions, documented enterprise case studies, and high-quality analyst research. Avoid using vendor marketing as the sole evidence for a strategic claim.
